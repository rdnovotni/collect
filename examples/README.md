# Examples

These records are illustrative. The publisher, series, and card numbers are fictional; they exist to demonstrate the format.

- `postcard/` — one record per file, covering every layer. Note how cards 7421 and 9102 share one **work** (the same photograph, reused), 7421 has a green-back **variant**, and two **instances** show a cataloged card and an uncataloged real-photo postcard.
- `sports-card/` — a base **catalog** card, a **variant** for a numbered parallel, a graded **instance** of that parallel, and an uncataloged autographed relic **instance** described entirely with its own fields.
- `coin/` — a **catalog** coin type/date/mint combination and a graded (Sheldon-scale) **instance** of it with a mint mark and die variety.
- `stamp/` — a **catalog** record for a classic imperforate issue, and an uncataloged, postmarked **instance** described entirely with its own fields.
- `package/` — the same records bundled as a Collect package.
- `csv/my-postcards.csv` — a collector's spreadsheet that converts to valid records with `collect csv2json`.

Image files referenced by the examples are not included.
